## Do I need a commercial license?

You **don't** need one if you:

- run WebHaat (modified or not) for your own store and publish any changes you make to its source code, or
- run an unmodified copy, or
- pay us for setup or maintenance of a store that stays under the AGPL.

You **do** need one if you want to:

- sell or host WebHaat as part of a closed-source product or SaaS platform (for example, a multi-store "shop builder" for your customers) without releasing your changes, or
- ship modified versions to clients without giving them the source code under the AGPL, or
- remove the license and copyright notices.
