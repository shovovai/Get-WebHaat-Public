# Trademark Policy

The source code of WebHaat is open source under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE). The **name "WebHaat" and the WebHaat logo** are not part of that license.

## What you can do

- Say that your store is "built with WebHaat" or "powered by WebHaat".
- Keep the "Powered by WebHaat" footer credit, or hide it with `NEXT_PUBLIC_SHOW_POWERED_BY=false`.
- Refer to WebHaat by name in articles, tutorials and reviews.

## What needs permission

- Offering setup, hosting or maintenance services **under the WebHaat name** ("WebHaat Official Setup", "WebHaat Cloud", and so on).
- Using "WebHaat" in the name of your product, company or domain.
- Using the logo in a way that suggests your business is the official WebHaat team.

If you fork the project and change it significantly, please give your fork a different name.

Questions: contact the maintainer through the sales website or the GitHub profile of this repository.
